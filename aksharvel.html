The problem

A child starts school in a language they do not speak at home.

AT HOME
वारली

The language of the family, the village, and everything a young child already knows how to say.

AT SCHOOL
मराठी

The language of the classroom, the textbook and the examination. An unfamiliar script, from the first day of Grade 1.

The gap shows up first in reading. A child who cannot yet decode a single letter cannot decode a word built from it. In a classroom of fifty, there is rarely time for one child to practise aloud, one letter at a time, until it sticks. That practice is what Aksharvel gives them.

Who it is for

Built for Grade 1–6 learners in Government ZP and Ashram schools across Palghar.

Aksharvel is made with Nplusone Social Foundation for roughly 20,000 learners in tribal and multilingual parts of Maharashtra, supported by community facilitators and teachers.

How it works

Four ways to meet the same letter.

Recognising a letter, forming it and saying it are different skills. Aksharvel practises each of them, and never asks a child to read an instruction in order to begin.

Try it

The actual sound a child hears.

Every letter in the app has two recordings: one at normal speed and one slowed down for a child who needs it. These are the real files, taken straight from the app.

Tap any letter

Fifteen of the fifty letters are here. The app teaches all of them, vowels and consonants, including the joined letters क्ष, त्र and ज्ञ.

Tap a letter to hear it. Check your device volume.

How to use it

From download to first letter, in about two minutes.

Aksharvel asks for no account and no setup. A child can be practising their first letter within a minute of the app opening.

९:४१▮▮▮
Animated walkthrough

Opening the app

No login and no setup. The app opens on the learning path, so the phone can go straight to a child.

An illustration of the interface, not a screen recording. A real recording of the app in use will replace this.

1

Install it

Download the APK and open it. Android will ask whether to allow installing from this source; say yes. No Play Store account is needed.

Android 7.0 or newer. Around 250 MB, because the speech model travels inside the app.
2

Open it and hand it over

There is no login, no profile and no setup screen. The app opens straight onto the learning path, so you can pass the phone to a child immediately.

3

Tap a letter on the path

The winding path shows the letters in order. Nothing is locked, so a child can open any letter at any time rather than being held at one they find hard.

4

Listen, then answer

The letter plays by itself. The big speaker button repeats it and the tortoise button plays it slowly. The child taps the letter they heard, or drags it into place.

Three tries per question. After that the answer is shown, and the lesson moves on.
5

Try writing and speaking

The pencil tab asks the child to trace the letter with a finger and marks the stroke. The microphone tab asks them to say it aloud and checks the pronunciation on the phone itself.

Grant the microphone permission once, the first time you open the speaking tab.
6

Close it whenever

Progress, stars and the daily streak are saved after every question, so a ten minute turn on a shared phone still counts. Put the phone in airplane mode and nothing changes.
